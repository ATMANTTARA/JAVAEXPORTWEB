EXPORT DOCUMENTATION &
COMPLIANCE

1

Commercial Invoice

Itemized pricing, quantities, and payment terms formatted to destination country requirements, including Harmonized System codes for classification.

2

Packing List

Detailed container contents, package dimensions, weights, and marking specifications — essential for warehouse handling and inventory management.

3

Certificate of Origin

Indonesian Chamber of Commerce documentation verifying product origin, required for preferential tariff treatment under trade agreements.

4

Bill of Lading

Title document for goods transfer. Negotiable or straight B/L options available based on payment terms and customer requirements.

5

Wood Legality Certificate

SVLK (Sistem Verifikasi Legalitas Kayu) documentation or equivalent forestry compliance certificates, proving legal harvesting and processing.

6

Phytosanitary Certificate

Plant health inspection approval when required by the destination country, confirming absence of pests and compliance with agricultural regulations.

LEGAL & ENVIRONMENTAL COMPLIANCE

Required Documentation

  • Company business license (AKTA/NIB/NPWP)
  • Export permit (API-U)
  • SVLK legality certificates for wood products
  • Food-grade finish certifications when applicable
  • Material safety documentation
  • Fumigation certificates

Compliance Standards

All products meet relevant safety and environmental regulations for target markets. We provide documentation supporting compliance with Japanese JAS, European EUTR, and American Lacey Act requirements.

Food-Grade Finishes

Kitchenware items utilize non-toxic, food-safe finishes compliant with FDA or EU food contact material regulations. Natural teak oils or certified food-grade varnishes are applied.

Sustainable Practices

Partner manufacturers follow responsible forestry practices, with documentation available demonstrating legal wood sourcing and environmental management systems.
